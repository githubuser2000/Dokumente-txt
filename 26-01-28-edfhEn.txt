* Three Predicate-Logic Elements – Classical Metrical Feet (8 = 2³)
  Existence as *finding* rather than determination, phenomenon instead of time ⏳✨

| Nr | Syllables | Pattern | Name          | Comment / Comparison |
|----+-----------+---------+---------------+----------------------|
| 1  | 2         | ˘ ˘     | Pyrrhichius   | completely soft, unstable 🌫️ |
| 2  | 2         | ˘ ´     | Iamb          | rising, speech, drama 🎭 |
| 3  | 2         | ´ ˘     | Trochee       | falling, song, emphasis 🎶 |
| 4  | 2         | ´ ´     | Spondee       | maximally hard, weight ⚖️ |
| 5  | 3         | ˘ ´ ˘   | Amphibrach   | symmetric, floating ⚖️✨ |
| 6  | 3         | ´ ˘ ˘   | Dactyl       | solemn, epic 🏛️ |
| 7  | 3         | ˘ ˘ ´   | Anapaest     | driving forward 🚀 |
| 8  | 3         | ´ ´ ´   | Molossus     | extremely hard, rhetorical 🧱 |

- Iamb: “soft on the first, hard on the second” → like a step forward 🚶‍♂️➡️  
- Trochee: “hard on the first, soft on the second” → like a step downward ⬇️  
- Dactyl: “hard, soft, soft” → a small downward motion with momentum 🌊  
- Iamb / Trochee → linear movement (forward vs. downward) ➡️⬇️  
- Dactyl / Anapaest → directed dynamics (fall vs. jump) 🎢  
- Amphibrach → symmetry, balance ⚖️  
- Pyrrhichius / Molossus / Spondee → limit cases (too soft / too hard) ❄️🔥
